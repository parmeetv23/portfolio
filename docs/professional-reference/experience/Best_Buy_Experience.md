## Overview

Worked full-time as a Seasonal Sales Associate in the computing department at Best Buy Canada's Deerfoot Meadows location in Calgary, Alberta, from September 2025 to January 2026. The role was focused on helping customers choose computers, accessories, and related technology products while working toward individual sales expectations during the busy holiday retail season.

Assessed customer needs and budgets; explained and compared computer specifications; recommended suitable products; and offered relevant additional merchandise, Best Buy Memberships, Geek Squad Protection plans, and services. The selling environment placed considerable emphasis on completing sales, attaching products and services, and increasing transaction value alongside meeting customer needs.

Primarily worked in computing, but assisted elsewhere when possible, including with headphones, cameras, and retrieving other merchandise. Supported store operations by checking stock, retrieving computers from the warehouse, restocking customer-facing merchandise, and updating prices when required. Worked during Black Friday, Boxing Day, and the wider holiday shopping period.

- Helped customers select laptops, desktops, monitors, peripherals, and related computing products.
- Recommended products for school, work, gaming, and everyday computing needs.
- Compared competing products and explained why particular options better matched customer requirements.
- Recommended alternatives when the customer's initial choice was not the best fit.
